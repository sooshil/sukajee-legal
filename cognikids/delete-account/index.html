Delete your CogniKids account

CogniKids by Sukajee · Last updated: October 30, 2026

A parent account is optional, so you may not have one. If you do, you can delete it, and everything stored with it, in either of these ways.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open CogniKids and go to the parent area (enter your parent PIN).
  2. Open the menu and choose Parent account.
  3. Scroll to Delete account and tap Delete account….
  4. Confirm with your password (or choose your Google account again) and tap Delete for good.

Without the app (by email)

Email info@sukajee.com from the email address of the account, with the subject "Delete my CogniKids account". We delete the account and its data within 30 days and reply to confirm. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address first, so that nobody else can delete your account.

What is deleted

  • The parent account: email address and sign-in details.
  • The cloud backup of every child: profiles (display name, avatar, grade), progress, sessions, answers and settings.
  • Problem reports sent from the account.

Nothing is kept, apart from copies that may remain in Google's own system backups for a short period before they are overwritten.

What stays

Progress saved on your devices stays there, because the app works without an account. To remove it too, delete each child in Parent area → Children, or uninstall the app.
